## 1. Executive Summary

During the Semester 1 platform verification of the SHOPIVO administrative console, a data-binding discrepancy was identified between the backend metrics contract and two administrative reporting interfaces:
- [`Analytics.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/SREEJA/Documents/Fraud Detection/secure-commerce-ai/frontend/src/pages/admin/Analytics.jsx)
- [`Reports.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/SREEJA/Documents/Fraud Detection/secure-commerce-ai/frontend/src/pages/admin/Reports.jsx)

While the primary administrative dashboard ([`Dashboard.jsx`](file:///c:/Users/SREEJA/Documents/Fraud Detection/secure-commerce-ai/frontend/src/pages/admin/Dashboard.jsx)) correctly referenced backend properties, `Analytics.jsx` and `Reports.jsx` referenced non-existent or misaligned fields such as `totalRevenue`, `completedOrders`, and `activeUsers`. As a result:
1. Gross Merchandise Value rendered as `₹0` or `NaN`.
2. Average Order Value (AOV) calculated incorrectly as `₹0` or failed due to zero-division guards.
3. Order fulfillment indicators and customer/merchant reports displayed empty or inaccurate values.
4. Exported CSV reports generated by the Reports module contradicted displayed data and contained mismatched metric names.

**Remediation:**  
The frontend bindings have been corrected to strictly bind against the authoritative backend DTOs (`AdminOrderSummaryMetricsResponse` and `AdminUserSummaryMetricsResponse`). Financial terminology has been standardized to **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)** and **Total Order Value**, preventing misrepresentation of non-cancelled order volume as collected cash, net revenue, or company profit.

Zero backend code, database schemas, payment integrations, or refund handlers were altered. Vite production build passed in 657ms with 0 compilation errors, and Oxlint passed with 0 errors across 101 files.

## 6. Financial and Architectural Principles Clarification

1. **Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) vs. Net Revenue:**  
   In multi-vendor e-commerce architectures (such as SHOPIVO), `totalOrderValue` represents the total value of non-cancelled merchandise booked on the platform. Because SHOPIVO operates a multi-vendor marketplace where merchant payouts, COD settlements, and gateway processing occur, labeling `totalOrderValue` as "Net Revenue", "Profit", or "Cash Collected" would be financially inaccurate. The label **"Gross Merchandise Value (GMV)"** is standard and truthful.

2. **Fulfillment Pipeline Transparency:**  
   By mapping `deliveredOrders` directly to "Completed / Delivered" rather than an ambiguous "completedOrders", administrators have clear visibility into orders that have physically reached customers versus those pending in fulfillment (`pendingOrders`, `processingOrders`, `shippedOrders`).

3. **Merchant vs. User Community Separation:**  
   Clarifying `activeSellers` as "Active Merchants" ensures administrators do not confuse active vendor stores with customer login activity.
